Architecture note:
  All builds are for aarch64 (ARM64). The decbench-compile image runs natively
  on arm64 Docker (host: Apple Silicon). x86-64 results may differ in function
  count and collision rate due to different NEON/SSE codepaths and alignment.

Collision measurement methodology (scripts/measure_collisions.py):
  - Directly implements DecBench binfmt.die_attr_owner resolution.
  - Collision identity key: Resolved DW_AT_name (following DW_AT_specification
    and DW_AT_abstract_origin chains).
  - Diagnostic display: Demangled DW_AT_linkage_name.
  - Project scope: DW_AT_decl_file read from DWARF line program headers,
    excluding /usr/include, /usr/lib, /include/c++/, bits/, and ext/.
